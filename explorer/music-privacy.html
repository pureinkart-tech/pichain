AI Music Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Overview

AI Music Studio ("the App") is a music creation and editing tool developed by PIChain. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Audio files you upload: Temporarily stored on our servers for processing (stem separation, transcription, effects, etc.). Files are automatically deleted after 6 hours.
  • Songs you generate: Stored in your session history. You can delete them at any time.
  • Published songs: If you choose to publish a song, it becomes publicly accessible via a share link.
  • Comments and ratings: Stored with an author name you provide (can be anonymous).

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not require account registration or login
  • We do not use cookies for tracking
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs

Audio Processing

All audio processing (AI generation, stem separation, effects) happens on our GPU servers. Uploaded audio is processed in memory and temporary files are cleaned up automatically. We do not listen to, analyze, or store your audio beyond what is needed for the requested operation.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Cloudflare for content delivery and DDoS protection. Cloudflare may collect standard web traffic logs (IP addresses, request timestamps). See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

  • Uploaded files: Deleted after 6 hours
  • Generated songs: Kept until you delete them
  • Published songs: Kept until you unpublish them

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
