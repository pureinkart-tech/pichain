Quantum Resistance Stack

PIChain is the first L1 blockchain with comprehensive quantum resistance across signatures, addresses, mining, consensus, and network transport.

Signatures
ML-DSA-65 (NIST FIPS 204, lattice-based) + SLH-DSA-SHAKE-128f (NIST FIPS 205, hash-based). Both must verify. Redundant quantum safety — two independent mathematical families.
Addresses
Quantum-safe hash combiner: Blake3 XOR SHA-3-256. If either hash function is preimage-resistant, addresses are safe. Two completely different constructions (ChaCha-based vs Keccak sponge).
Mining
VDF hash chain gates proof submission rate — inherently sequential, no quantum speedup possible. Progressive strengthening: starts accessible (100K iterations), hardens over time (4M+ at maturity). Sqrt reward scaling caps hardware advantages to ≤1.5x.
Future-Proof
Crypto version field enables scheme upgrades without hard forks. STARK aggregation framework ready for block-level proof compression. Progressive strengthening auto-hardens VDF, mining caps, and reward scaling as the chain matures.
Consensus
ML-DSA-65 validator signatures replace BLS12-381 for quantum-safe consensus. Per-validator signing with domain separation and chain ID binding prevents cross-chain replay. Bullshark DAG + Avalanche fast-path for sub-second finality.
Network
Hybrid key exchange (ML-KEM + X25519) protects P2P traffic against harvest-now-decrypt-later attacks. PQ validator announcements with SLH-DSA key commitments. Eclipse-resistant peer scoring with minimum peer diversity enforcement.
