How Custom Bonus Works

Bonuses let you fine-tune who appears in your feed based on traits like height, income, ethnicity, lifestyle, and personality — without changing anyone's actual rating.

+ Boost (up to 3 pts)

Lifts people with traits you prefer up into your visible range. For example, if you're rated 6 (you see 5–7), giving +2 for tall people means a tall person rated 4 appears as a 6 to you — now in your range.

- Reduce (up to 3 pts)

Pushes people with certain traits out of your visible range. A person rated 6.5 with your -2 penalty drops to 4.5 — below your floor, so they disappear from your feed.

Important
  • Your upper ceiling (rating + 1) never changes — you can't see people above it
  • Bonuses only affect matching & discovery, not anyone's real rating
  • Other users don't know about your bonus settings
  • Ratings shown as: ★ 4.0 (+2.0) = 6.0
